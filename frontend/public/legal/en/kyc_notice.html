Identity Verification (KYC) Notice

Updated 23 October 2026

1. What this notice is

1.1. Nothing can be bought in the Cabinet at aivis.one/app/ before you have passed identity verification, called KYC ("know your customer"). This notice tells you what you send, what it costs, who checks it, what happens to it and what follows. It is read together with the Cabinet user agreement and the privacy policy.

2. Who is responsible

2.1. The controller of the data you send is AIVIS.ONE LLC, a limited liability company of Georgia, identification number 404804422, legal address Georgia, Tbilisi, Mtatsminda district, village Okrokana, Akaki Tsereteli str., N14 (the "Company").

2.2. You can write to the director, Artem Baliakno, at legal@aivis.one.

3. When verification is needed

3.1. Anyone may register, enter the Cabinet and look inside without verification.

3.2. A passed verification is needed for a purchase, an instalment, a payout of money and an application to become an agent-integrator.

3.3. A person who receives parts as a gift of the Company passes the same paid verification.

3.4. A USDT deposit is accepted before verification, and the verification fee may itself be paid in USDT.

3.5. Once your verification is passed, support may give you the bank details of the Company, at support's decision. No bank transfer is accepted from Russian buyers, a Russian resident in the European Union included: they pay in USDT and are not given bank details.

4. What you send

4.1. You send:

  • the type of your identity document: a passport, an ID card or a driving licence;
  • a photograph of the front of the document and, except for a passport, of its back;
  • a photograph of yourself.

4.2. Each file must be a JPEG or PNG image of up to 10 MB. Send only the document you are verified by, and only your own.

5. How it is checked, and why

5.1. The Company's support staff review every submission by hand and decide. No automated decision is taken.

5.2. Staff screen you against the sanctions lists of the United States, the European Union, the United Kingdom and the United Nations. The Company refuses persons on these lists and residents of Cuba, Iran, North Korea, Syria, Crimea, DNR and LNR. Russian and Belarusian citizens are accepted after screening against the lists.

5.3. The result is that you are verified (approved) or not (rejected). A verification may later be withdrawn (revoked), for example where the documents turn out to be false or where the Company's screening policy requires it. An agent-integrator whose verification is withdrawn is paid nothing until he passes it again.

5.4. We use your files to confirm who you are and that we may deal with you under the Company's screening policy, which includes the check against the sanctions lists. The basis is our legitimate interest in knowing who we sell to, our duty of care in payments, and your request to buy.

6. The fee

6.1. Verification is paid. The fee is 10 USD for each attempt.

6.2. The fee is never returned, whatever the result and whether or not you go on to buy anything, because it pays for a service that has been rendered.

6.3. If a verification is rejected or withdrawn, a new verification is a new attempt and needs a new fee. Once you are approved, you pay nothing more for it.

6.4. If you make a USDT deposit but do not pass verification, the Company refunds the deposit within 90 days, less the verification fee where that fee was taken from this deposit. The refund goes to the sending address only and is made less the network fee. Money received from a person found on any sanctions list the Company applies is not refunded in this way: it is frozen in the account, the Financial Monitoring Service of Georgia is notified, and nothing moves until it instructs; the freeze ends as clause 6.4 of the Company's AML and Screening Policy says.

7. Who sees your documents

7.1. Only the Company's staff can open your documents, and the links by which they open them stop working after a few minutes.

7.2. The files are kept on the Company's own server. Cloudflare carries them as a proxy while they travel to the server. The privacy policy names the others who handle data for the Cabinet.

8. How long we keep them

8.1. If you hold a share, we keep your documents and photographs as long as your register entry: while the share exists and for 10 years after it ends. The basis is the buyer agreement and then our legitimate interest in proving the right and every payment.

8.2. If you never held a share, we keep your documents and photographs while your account exists and delete them when it is closed, except what the law requires us to keep. The privacy policy states the rule for all data.

9. Your rights

9.1. You have the rights set out in the privacy policy, including to see what we hold about you and to ask us to correct, delete or block it. Where the law requires us to keep a record, we keep it and tell you so.

10. Changes and language

10.1. When we change this notice, we replace the text and show the line "Updated" with the date at the top.

10.2. The English text of this notice prevails over its text in any other language.
