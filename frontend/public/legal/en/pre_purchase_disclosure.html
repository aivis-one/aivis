Pre-Purchase Disclosure Sheet

Updated 23 October 2026

Issued by AIVIS.ONE LLC, a limited liability company of Georgia, identification number 404804422, legal address Georgia, Tbilisi, Mtatsminda district, village Okrokana, Akaki Tsereteli str., N14 (the "Company").

This sheet is shown to you before you buy. Read it before you accept the buyer agreement.

1. What you buy

1.1. You buy parts of one project of AIVIS.ONE LLC. What the Company sells you is an existing right that it owns: a share of the pool of the project's gross receipts. Gross receipts are all money received from customers for the project's product, before any deduction.

1.2. Your right is an entry in the Company's register of the project's IP. Your Cabinet holds the proof of the entry.

1.3. A part is not a share of profit, not a share or equity in the Company, not an option and not a fund. No profit is computed.

2. The pool and the commission

2.1. The holders of a project's parts share a fixed pool of 15 percent of its gross receipts. The Company's service commission is 10 percent of the pool, on top: the pool is 16.67 percent of gross receipts, of which 1.67 percent goes to the Company and 15 percent to all parts.

2.2. Your share is 15 percent of the project's gross receipts x your parts / 1,000,000,000.

2.3. Example. Gross receipts of 100,000 for a quarter: the pool is 16,667, the Company's commission 1,667, all parts 15,000, and 1 percent of the parts 150.

2.4. The pool may only be raised, from 15 percent up, and the commission may only be lowered, from 10 percent down, by the Company alone. Any other change applies to parts already sold only with the holder's consent.

3. The risks

3.1. A project's pool is zero until its product sells. Until then you receive nothing from the pool.

3.2. A project may not reach revenue. It may not reach the cutoff of 25,000 USD a month of gross receipts, and it may not be formed into a company of its own working under the licence.

3.3. The Company holds every part that is not yet sold or given. The pool income of those parts is directed to developing the projects.

3.4. The money from selling parts is never gross receipts. It goes to the project's budget by its roadmap and business-financial model, less the selling cost.

4. The selling cost

4.1. The Company has no sales department of its own. Sales are made by agent-integrators, whose commissions are the selling cost. They are paid from the money for the parts, the money of an index purchase included, and come to up to 17 percent of a purchase at full levels.

5. The price

5.1. The price of a part is the project's fair valuation divided by 1,000,000,000.

5.2. Every project is revalued at least every six months until its product first sells, and at least every three months from then. A project's value is never below 1,000,000 USD.

6. Who pays you

6.1. The Company pays the holders itself, as the one debtor. It pays quarterly, and only after the 14 days of withdrawal. An amount under 10 USD is carried forward to the next quarter.

6.2. The Company may sell a project's IP only after it has bought back every holder's share. The buy-out binds every holder, you included: your share then passes to the Company at a price not below its value at the project's last revaluation, and you are paid for it.

7. The index

7.1. The index is the averaged choice across the projects it holds.

7.2. The money of an index purchase, less the same selling cost, goes in equal shares to the index's projects. The Company decides nothing about it. A buyer who wants one project to develop buys that project.

8. Your consumer rights

8.1. Every buyer is treated as a consumer.

8.2. You may withdraw within 14 days of the purchase, through support. A part joins the pool only when the money paid for it can no longer be recalled, that is after those 14 days.

8.3. Disputes go to the state courts of Georgia in Tbilisi, and your protection as a consumer at your home is kept.

8.4. If you are unhappy with a purchase, an accrual or a payment, write to support in the Cabinet. If support does not settle it, write to the Company at its address at the top of this sheet.

8.5. With each payment you receive a statement of your accruals and payments. It is the Company's report to you after the sale.
