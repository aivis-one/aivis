Agent-Integrator Agreement

Updated 25 October 2026

Made by acceptance in the Cabinet at aivis.one/app/; the Cabinet records the date and time of acceptance.

between

AIVIS.ONE LLC, a limited liability company of Georgia, identification number 404804422, legal address Georgia, Tbilisi, Mtatsminda district, village Okrokana, Akaki Tsereteli str., N14 (the "Company"),

and

the natural person whose verification (KYC) is passed, whose application the Company has approved and who accepts this agreement in the Cabinet from his own account (the "Agent-Integrator").

1. Subject

1.1. The Agent-Integrator sells parts of the projects of AIVIS.ONE LLC (the "Parts") outside Georgia and supports the three levels below him, and the Company pays him for it on the terms of this agreement.

1.2. The seller of the Parts is the Company. Every purchase is made by the buyer in the Cabinet under the buyer agreement, which the buyer accepts himself, and the Company shows the buyer the pre-purchase disclosure sheet before the purchase.

1.3. The Agent-Integrator supports his three levels as a whole: he trains their agent-integrators, tells them and their buyers about the projects and their products, answers their questions about the projects and the Cabinet, keeps them informed in time, reminds them of payments where needed and helps them through verification.

2. Becoming an agent-integrator

2.1. An agent-integrator is a natural person. Anyone registered in the Cabinet may apply, but the application is closed until the applicant's verification is passed.

2.2. The Company's staff decide on the application. An approved applicant accepts this agreement by a click in the Cabinet.

2.3. This agreement and the Agent-Integrator's transactions are downloadable in the Cabinet.

3. Independent contractor

3.1. The Agent-Integrator is an independent contractor. He is not an employee, partner or representative of the Company, he has no authority to bind it, and he is not on its payroll. He bears his own costs and risks.

3.2. The Agent-Integrator answers for any licence or registration that his country requires for his work.

4. Buyers

4.1. A buyer is attributed to the Agent-Integrator by the link of the Agent-Integrator through which the buyer registered in the Cabinet.

4.2. The three levels below the Agent-Integrator are counted by the chain of registration links through agent-integrators, exactly three: the first level is the buyers who registered by his own link, the second level is the buyers who registered by the link of an agent-integrator of his first level, and the third level is the buyers who registered by the link of an agent-integrator of his second level.

4.3. The chain ends at an agent-integrator who is blocked in the Cabinet: neither he nor any agent-integrator above him is paid on the purchases made below him.

4.4. Nothing is bought before the buyer passes the paid verification with documents, checked by hand by the Company's support staff.

4.5. The Company's screening applies to every buyer, whoever brought him. The Company refuses persons on the sanctions lists of the United States, the European Union, the United Kingdom and the United Nations, and residents of Cuba, Iran, North Korea, Syria, Crimea, DNR and LNR. Russian and Belarusian citizens are accepted after screening against the lists.

4.6. No bank transfer is accepted from Russian buyers, a Russian resident in the European Union included. They pay in USDT and are not given bank details.

4.7. The Agent-Integrator tells buyers the plain state of the projects and promises them nothing that the Company has not published.

5. Money commission

5.1. The Company pays the Agent-Integrator a money commission on the purchases of Parts made by the buyers of the three levels below him: 10 percent of the money paid for the Parts at the first level, 3 percent at the second level and 1 percent at the third level.

5.2. The commission is paid in money, from the money paid for the Parts.

5.3. On an instalment purchase the commission accrues on the money of each monthly payment that is paid.

5.4. Where a buyer withdraws from a purchase within the 14 days or his payment is reversed, the Company's support cancels the commission of every agent-integrator from that purchase before it is paid.

5.5. The commissions of the agent-integrators are the selling cost of the Parts and are disclosed to the buyer before the purchase.

6. Volume pools

6.1. Each month the 20 agent-integrators with the largest first-line volume of that month share 2 percent of all the month's purchases, gifts excluded.

6.2. Each quarter the 10 agent-integrators with the largest first-line volume of its three months share 1 percent of all the quarter's purchases, gifts excluded.

6.3. An agent-integrator's first-line volume is the purchases of the buyers of his first level (clause 4.2), those who registered by his own link. The two pools are counted separately, and each pool is shared among its agent-integrators in proportion to each one's volume; one agent-integrator may share in both.

6.4. The volume of a purchase withdrawn within the 14 days, or whose payment is reversed, is left out of the pools.

7. Bonus Parts

7.1. On a one-time purchase by a buyer of his first level (clause 4.2), one who registered by his own link, the Agent-Integrator receives bonus Parts by the amount of that purchase: 5 percent from 200 USD, 10 percent from 1,000 USD, 15 percent from 5,000 USD and 20 percent from 25,000 USD.

7.2. The Company's support credits the bonus Parts by hand from the bonus fund of the project, 14 percent of its Parts, after the 14 days in which the buyer may withdraw from the purchase.

7.3. The Agent-Integrator holds his bonus Parts on the terms of the buyer agreement, and the Company enters him in the register of the project as their holder. Bonus Parts carry the same share of the project's distributable profit as any other Part.

8. The 14-day hold and payouts

8.1. The Agent-Integrator's commission accrues when the money for the purchase is paid. A payout he asks for is paid 14 days after his request: the Company's support holds it for these 14 days, the same 14 days in which a buyer may withdraw from a purchase.

8.2. The Agent-Integrator has a separate withdrawal account under this agreement. It is the Company's debt for the commission he has earned, and it is paid after the 14 days.

8.3. The Agent-Integrator asks for a payout in the Cabinet, and the Company's support staff process it. His commission may be paid in USDT through the Cabinet or by bank in USD or EUR through support.

8.4. The Company approves every payout and does not pay a request that the balance of the withdrawal account no longer covers after a cancellation under clause 5.4.

8.5. A payout needs a passed verification. An Agent-Integrator whose verification is withdrawn is paid nothing until he passes it again, and his accruals are kept.

9. Ending

9.1. This agreement has no fixed term. Either party may end it by giving 30 days' written notice.

9.2. The Company may end this agreement at once where the Agent-Integrator breaks it, where his verification is withdrawn, or where the Company's screening policy or a sanctions rule requires it.

9.3. When this agreement ends, the balance of the withdrawal account that has not been cancelled is paid to the Agent-Integrator on his request through the Company's support, 14 days after the request. The Company's support pays an Agent-Integrator who is blocked in the Cabinet by hand.

10. Liability

10.1. To the extent Georgian law allows, and except in cases of intent or gross negligence, each party is liable to the other only for direct loss. The Company does not promise the Agent-Integrator any income.

11. Taxes

11.1. The Company settles its own taxes. The Agent-Integrator pays his own taxes and settles them with his tax authority.

12. Forum

12.1. A dispute under this agreement goes to the state courts of Georgia in Tbilisi.

12.2. The Agent-Integrator may sign a separate agreement for arbitration at GIAC after a dispute arises.

12.3. This agreement is governed by the law of Georgia.

13. Language and general

13.1. The English text of this agreement prevails over its text in any other language.

13.2. How the Agent-Integrator's data is handled is set out in the privacy policy.
