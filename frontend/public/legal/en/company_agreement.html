Company Agreement

Updated 23 October 2026

Made by acceptance in the Cabinet at aivis.one/app/ at the first entry into the project's account; the Cabinet records the date and time of acceptance.

between

AIVIS.ONE LLC, a limited liability company of Georgia, identification number 404804422, legal address Georgia, Tbilisi, Mtatsminda district, village Okrokana, Akaki Tsereteli str., N14 (the "Company"),

and

the company for which the Company's staff have opened an account in the Cabinet for one of the projects of AIVIS.ONE LLC, and which accepts this agreement at its first entry into that account (the "Account Holder").

1. Subject

1.1. The Company opens for the Account Holder one account in the Cabinet for one project of AIVIS.ONE LLC (the "Project Account").

1.2. Through the Project Account the Account Holder publishes the project's posts, roadmap and documents.

1.3. The intellectual property of the project is the Company's. The seller of the parts of the project is the Company.

1.4. This agreement and the documents accepted in the Project Account are downloadable in the Cabinet.

2. The parts of the project

2.1. The project has 1,000,000,000 parts. The number is fixed and no part is ever added.

2.2. The parts are divided as follows: 25 percent for sale, 10 percent index fund, 14 percent bonus fund, and 51 percent reserve and the Company's own. The Project Account shows this division and does not change it.

3. The price of a part

3.1. The price of a part is not entered by hand in the Project Account. It is the project's fair valuation divided by 1,000,000,000, the same approach for every project.

3.2. The project is revalued at least every six months until its product first sells, and at least every three months from then, justified by its formulas.

3.3. The project's value is never below 1,000,000 USD, which is its base until another valuation is made. News, work done or a deal move the price only through such a revaluation.

4. The pool and the money of the project

4.1. The holders of the project's parts share a fixed pool of 15 percent of the project's gross receipts. Gross receipts are all money received from customers for the project's product, before any deduction. Until the project's product sells, its pool is zero.

4.2. The money from selling the project's parts goes to the project's budget by its roadmap and business-financial model, less the selling cost. It is never gross receipts.

4.3. The Company pays the holders of the project's parts itself. It is their one debtor, and the duty to pay them does not pass to the Account Holder or to anyone else.

5. Forum

5.1. A dispute under this agreement goes to the state courts of Georgia in Tbilisi.

5.2. This agreement is governed by the law of Georgia.

6. Language

6.1. The English text of this agreement prevails over its text in any other language.
