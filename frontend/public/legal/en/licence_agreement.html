2. When this agreement applies

We plan the demo version for November 2026 and the working version, with licences on sale, from 1 January 2027. These dates are a plan, not a promise. This agreement applies to a purchase made once licences are on sale. Before then no licence is sold and no payment is taken in advance.

3. The Licence

The Licence follows the size that the Buyer declares at the purchase, which is declared once. The Company never counts the Buyer’s agents, runs or processes, and never limits them.

SANKORD runs inside the Buyer’s walls, on the Buyer’s own servers or in the Buyer’s own cloud account. Connections to services that have an API come with the Licence; there is no paid integration. Inside a company that has a Licence, another workplace may be added on the terms shown in the Cabinet.

When the Licence has been paid for in full, it is perpetual: it is the Buyer’s for good. A missed payment switches nothing off: the path to the perpetual Licence simply waits for the next payment. The Company keeps the ownership of the software and of the names SANKORD and AIVIS.ONE; the Licence gives the right to use and nothing more.

A Buyer who is paying receives the major versions and the integrator’s support. A Buyer who is not paying receives security fixes only. Security fixes are free for every version. New versions are the Buyer’s choice.

4. Price and payment

The price of the Licence and the terms of payment are those shown in the Cabinet at the purchase and agreed in the order. This agreement sets neither. Payments are made by the means the Cabinet offers and are recorded in the Buyer’s account. The Licence is not a subscription.

5. Verification and AML

A purchase can be made only after the Buyer has passed identity verification, as the KYC notice describes. The Company may refuse a sale, or hold or reverse a payment, where its AML policy requires it.

6. Delivery and start

The Buyer starts on his own, with a guide agent that leads him step by step, or with an integrator. An integrator who sets up or supports SANKORD for the Buyer is an independent contractor, not a representative of the Company. What the integrator bills for his own work is a matter between him and the Buyer.

7. Support

While the Buyer is paying, his integrator answers him within two working days. Support works from an error file that the Buyer sends. Security fixes are free for every version. A Buyer who bought directly from the Company is supported by the Company’s own support, which answers within two working days.

8. Refund and mandatory rights

The demo version is the trial: the Buyer may try it before buying. Once SANKORD has been installed for the Buyer, the price is not refunded. A consumer keeps the right to withdraw from the purchase that the law gives him, and nothing in this agreement limits another right that the Buyer has by law and that cannot be waived.

9. Liability

To the extent Georgian law allows, and except in cases of intent or gross negligence, the Company is not liable for indirect loss arising from the Buyer’s use of SANKORD. Nothing in this agreement limits a liability that the law does not allow to be limited.

10. Governing law and disputes

This agreement is governed by the law of Georgia. A dispute with a consumer goes to the state courts of Tbilisi, Georgia, unless mandatory law where the consumer lives gives him the right to go to another forum. Any other dispute arising out of or in connection with this agreement is finally resolved by arbitration at the Georgian International Arbitration Centre in Tbilisi, in accordance with its arbitration rules.
