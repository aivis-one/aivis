1. Subject

1.1. The Company owns the intellectual property of each of its projects. Under this agreement the Company sells the Buyer, and the Buyer buys, parts of a project (the "Parts"), in the quantity, for the price and of the project stated in the purchase.

1.2. What the Company sells is an existing right that it owns: the right to a share of the pool of the gross receipts of the project, as clause 3 defines it. This agreement is a contract of its own kind: the sale of a right to a share of future income from one project's IP.

1.3. A Part is not a share of profit, not a share or equity in the Company, not a right to acquire a share of the Company, not an option and not a fund. No profit is computed.

1.4. A purchase of units in the index project "AIVIS Projects Index" is a purchase under this agreement and is governed also by the Index Rules, which say what a unit is. In that project the Parts are called units. What the Index Rules state of the index -- what a unit is and the income it gives, the number of units, the price of a unit, the bonus fund, the use of the money paid for units, and the buy-out and closing of a project in the index -- applies to that project in place of clauses 1.2, 2.4, 3.1, 3.3, 3.6, 6.1 to 6.3 and 6.5, of the first sentence of clause 5.8 and of the second and third sentences of clause 5.9.

2. The register entry

2.1. The Company keeps a register of the IP of each project: who holds which Parts and how the pool of the project's gross receipts is distributed.

2.2. The Buyer's right is the entry in this register. The Cabinet holds the proof of the entry.

2.3. After the purchase the Buyer is a holder of the Parts (the "Holder").

2.4. Each project has 1,000,000,000 Parts. The number is fixed and no Part is ever added.

3. The pool and the commission

3.1. The holders of a project's Parts share a fixed pool of 15 percent of the project's gross receipts. It is the same 15 percent for every project and for every Part.

3.2. Gross receipts are all money received from customers for the project's product, before any deduction. The pool is counted only on the sales of the project's product. The money from selling Parts is never gross receipts.

3.3. The Holder's share of the pool equals his Parts divided by 1,000,000,000, that is 15 percent of the project's gross receipts x his Parts / 1,000,000,000.

3.4. The Company's service commission is 10 percent of the pool, on top. The pool is therefore 16.67 percent of gross receipts (15 / 0.9): 1.67 percent goes to the Company and 15 percent to all Parts.

3.5. Example. Gross receipts of 100,000 for a quarter: the pool is 16,667, the Company's commission 1,667, all Parts 15,000, and 1 percent of the Parts 150.

3.6. Until the project's product sells, the project's gross receipts, and so its pool, are zero.

4. Changes to the pool and the commission

4.1. The pool may only be raised, from 15 percent up, and the commission may only be lowered, from 10 percent down, by the Company alone. Such a change applies to the Parts already sold.

4.2. Any other change to what this agreement promises about the pool, the commission and the payment calendar applies to Parts already sold only with the Holder's consent.

4.3. The pool and the commission are shown to the Buyer before the purchase.

5. Who pays, and when

5.1. The Company pays the Holders itself. It is the one debtor of the Holders, and the duty to pay them does not pass to anyone else.

5.2. The Company pays from a separate account of each project. Until a project's gross receipts reach 25,000 USD a month, the Company sells the project's product itself. At that cutoff the Company creates a subsidiary for the project, keeps the IP and licenses it to the subsidiary, which collects the gross receipts and sends the pool to the Company. The Company stays the one debtor, and nothing changes for the Holder.

5.3. Payments are quarterly, and only after the 14 days of withdrawal in clause 8. An amount under 10 USD is carried to the next quarter.

5.4. A Part joins the pool only when the money paid for it can no longer be recalled, that is after those 14 days.

5.5. The Company pays the Holder to his bank account or card. A Holder who paid in USDT is paid in USDT to his confirmed address; for a payment in USDT, the Holder names the network and the wallet in his payout details. A payout needs a passed verification.

5.6. Each payment comes with a statement of the Holder's accruals and payments. The Cabinet shows accruals and payments only. It shows no balance that the Holder can spend or withdraw.

5.7. The Company holds every Part not yet sold or given, and the pool income of those Parts is directed to developing the projects.

5.8. The money from selling Parts goes to the budget of that project by its roadmap and business-financial model, less the selling cost. The Company has no sales department of its own; sales are made by agent-integrators, whose commissions are the selling cost, paid from the money for the Parts and disclosed to the Buyer before the purchase.

5.9. The Company stays the one debtor of the Buyer whatever happens to a project's IP. The Company may sell a project's IP only after it has bought back the share of every Holder of that project, at a price not below the value of the share at the project's last revaluation (clause 6.2). The buy-out binds every Holder: on such a sale each share passes to the Company at that price, and the Holder is paid for it.

6. The price and bonus Parts

6.1. The price of a Part is the project's fair valuation divided by 1,000,000,000. The same approach applies to every project.

6.2. Every project is revalued at least every six months until its product first sells, and at least every three months from then, justified by its formulas.

6.3. A project's value is never below 1,000,000 USD, which is its base until another valuation is made. News, work done or a deal move a price only through such a revaluation.

6.4. On a one-time purchase the Buyer receives bonus Parts by the amount of that purchase: 0 percent from 200 USD, 5 percent from 1,000 USD, 10 percent from 5,000 USD and 20 percent from 25,000 USD. No bonus Parts are given on an instalment purchase.

6.5. Bonus Parts come from the bonus fund of the project, 14 percent of its Parts. A Holder's bonus Parts carry the same share of the pool as any other Part.

7. Purchase and acceptance

7.1. The Buyer accepts this agreement by a click in the Cabinet. The agreement and the Buyer's transactions are downloadable in the Cabinet.

7.2. Before the purchase the Company shows the Buyer the pre-purchase disclosure sheet.

7.3. A Buyer is a natural person after verification. Every Buyer is treated as a consumer and makes no declaration whether he is a consumer or a business.

7.4. The minimum purchase is 200 USD. An instalment purchase runs 6, 12 or 24 months on the fixed packages of 200, 1,000, 5,000 and 25,000 USD; any other amount or term only by agreement through support. No bonus Parts are given on an instalment purchase.

7.5. On an instalment purchase the price is paid in equal monthly payments; the last payment may differ by the remainder of rounding. Each paid month is a purchase agreement of its own and transfers that month's share of the Parts booked for the instalment purchase: on 6 months, 10 percent of the booked Parts for each of the first five months and 50 percent for the last; on 12 months, 5 percent for each of the first eleven months and 45 percent for the last; on 24 months, 3 percent for each of the first twenty-three months and 31 percent for the last.

7.6. Each monthly payment is taken from the Buyer's account in the Cabinet on its due date; a payment the account does not cover on that date is overdue. If the account covers it within 7 days of its due date, the instalment purchase continues. If it is not covered within those 7 days, the instalment purchase ends: the Parts of the months already paid stay the Buyer's, the booked Parts of the unpaid months go back to sale, and no penalty is charged.

8. Withdrawal within 14 days

8.1. The Buyer may withdraw from the purchase within 14 days, by this agreement and through support only.

8.2. On withdrawal the full price goes back to the Buyer's single account in the Cabinet, and the bonus Parts of that purchase are cancelled. On the Buyer's request, support refunds the account out by the means by which he paid, less the network fee for USDT.

9. No partnership, no vote

9.1. The Holder takes no part in managing the Company and no part in its obligations. The Holders are not partners of the Company.

9.2. The Holders have no vote, not even an advisory voice on the product.

10. The share

10.1. A share passes only by inheritance, or to the Company by the buy-out of clause 5.9. Nothing else can be done with the share.

10.2. The Company promises no buy-back at the Holder's request; the buy-out of clause 5.9 is made only before a sale of a project's IP.

11. Taxes

11.1. The Company settles its own taxes and the taxes of the project. The Holder settles his own taxes with his tax authority.

11.2. A payment to the Holder is not a royalty: it is a share of receipts and not a right to use the IP. The Company withholds only what the Tax Code makes compulsory.

12. Forum

12.1. A dispute under this agreement goes to the state courts of Georgia in Tbilisi. The Buyer's protection as a consumer at his home is kept.

12.2. The Buyer may sign a separate agreement for arbitration at GIAC after a dispute arises.

12.3. This agreement is governed by the law of Georgia.

13. Language and general

13.1. The English text of this agreement prevails over its text in any other language.

13.2. A change to this agreement applies to Parts already sold only as clause 4 allows: in the Holder's favour by the Company alone, any other change only with the Holder's consent.
