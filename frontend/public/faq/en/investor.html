Questions and Answers

1. How do I register?

You register in the Cabinet at aivis.one/app/ with your e-mail address or through Telegram, and you confirm the address or the sign-in. Anyone may register, enter the Cabinet and look inside without verification. (Cabinet user agreement, 2.3 and 5.1)

2. What do I need before I can buy?

Nothing can be bought before you pass identity verification (KYC) with documents. The Company's support staff check it by hand. A passed verification is also needed for an instalment and a payout of money. (Cabinet user agreement, 5.2 and 7.3)

3. What do I send for verification?

You send the type of your identity document (a passport, an ID card or a driving licence), a photograph of the front of the document and, except for a passport, of its back, and a photograph of yourself. Each file is a JPEG or PNG image of up to 10 MB. (KYC notice, 4.1 and 4.2)

4. What does verification cost?

The fee is 10 USD for each attempt. It is never returned, whatever the result and whether or not you go on to buy, because it pays for a service that has been rendered. If a verification is rejected or withdrawn, a new verification is a new attempt with a new fee; once you are approved, you pay nothing more for it. (KYC notice, 6.1 to 6.3)

5. Who cannot buy?

The Company refuses persons on the sanctions lists of the United States, the European Union, the United Kingdom and the United Nations, and residents of Cuba, Iran, North Korea, Syria, Crimea, DNR and LNR. Russian and Belarusian citizens are accepted after screening against the lists. No bank transfer is accepted from Russian buyers, a Russian resident in the European Union included: they pay in USDT and are not given bank details. (Cabinet user agreement, 6.1 to 6.3)

6. How do I top up my account?

In the Cabinet you top up in USDT on the TRC20, ERC20 or BSC20 network. A USDT top-up is accepted before verification, and the verification fee may be paid from it. USDT is credited to you as internal settlement units; the Company holds no crypto for you. You can also pay by bank transfer in USD or EUR through support: support gives the bank details only after your verification is passed, at its decision. (Cabinet user agreement, 7.1 to 7.3 and 7.5)

7. What if I top up but do not pass verification?

The Company refunds your USDT top-up within 90 days, less the verification fee where that fee was taken from this top-up. The refund goes to the sending address only, on the network by which the top-up came, and is made less the network fee. Money received from a person found on any sanctions list the Company applies is not refunded in this way: it is frozen in the account, the Financial Monitoring Service of Georgia is notified, and nothing moves until it instructs; the freeze ends as clause 6.4 of the Company's AML and Screening Policy says. (Cabinet user agreement, 7.4)

8. What is a part?

AIVIS.ONE LLC will own the exclusive right to the computer program and the other works of each project whose parts it sells while the schedule for the project is unsigned, and owns it from the signing; it sells parts of a project only after the schedule is signed. What it sells is a share in an existing right that it owns: a part is one billionth (1/1,000,000,000) of the exclusive right to the project, and all the parts of a project together equal 100 percent of the right. By buying parts you become a co-owner of the right in the amount of your parts and receive a share of the project's distributable profit. Your right is your share in the exclusive right, expressed by the entry in the Company's register of the project's IP, and the Cabinet holds the proof of the entry. Under the buyer agreement you pass the management of the right to the Company, and you have no vote. A part is not a share or equity in the Company, not an option and not a fund. Each project has 1,000,000,000 parts, and no part is ever added. (Buyer agreement, 1.1 to 1.3, 2.1 to 2.4, 9.1 and 9.3)

9. How is my income counted?

The Company and the holders of a project's parts share the project's distributable profit in proportion to their parts. The distributable profit is the project's gross receipts, all money received from customers for the project's product before any deduction, less the project's direct costs from a closed list: payment-system and bank fees; hosting, servers and AI models that directly serve the product; third-party licences inside the product; taxes on the project's revenue, such as VAT; direct support of the product's customers. There are no other deductions; the development, salaries and marketing of the project are not deductions. Your income is the distributable profit x your parts / 1,000,000,000. The Company takes no commission: it earns only as the holder of its own parts. If the direct costs of a quarter exceed the receipts, the distributable profit of that quarter is zero; you bear no loss and the loss is not carried forward. Until the project's product sells, the distributable profit is zero. A change in your favour (a cost removed from the list, an earlier payment) needs no consent from you, and you may ask support for the detail of a quarter's costs. (Buyer agreement, 3.1 to 3.4, 3.6 and 4.1)

10. How and when am I paid?

The Company pays the holders itself. Payments are quarterly, and only after the 14 days of withdrawal; an amount under 10 USD is carried to the next quarter. You are paid to your bank account or card; a holder who paid in USDT is paid in USDT to his confirmed address and names the network and the wallet in his payout details. A payout needs a passed verification. Each payment comes with a calculation of your share (the revenue, the direct costs by the list, the distributable profit and your share) and a statement of your accruals and payments. (Buyer agreement, 5.1, 5.3, 5.5 and 5.6; Cabinet user agreement, 9.3)

11. What is the minimum purchase, and are there bonus parts?

The minimum purchase is 200 USD. The price of a part is the project's fair valuation divided by 1,000,000,000. On a one-time purchase you receive bonus parts by its amount: 0 percent from 200 USD, 5 percent from 1,000 USD, 10 percent from 5,000 USD and 20 percent from 25,000 USD. No bonus parts are given on an instalment purchase. (Buyer agreement, 6.1, 6.4 and 7.4)

12. How does an instalment purchase work?

An instalment purchase runs 6, 12 or 24 months, on the fixed packages of 200, 1,000, 5,000 and 25,000 USD; any other amount or term only by agreement through support. The price is paid in equal monthly payments, and each paid month transfers its share of the parts booked for the purchase. Each payment is taken from your account in the Cabinet on its due date. If the account covers it within 7 days, the purchase continues; if not, it ends: the parts of the months already paid stay yours, the booked parts of the unpaid months go back to sale, and no penalty is charged. (Buyer agreement, 7.4 to 7.6)

13. Can I withdraw from a purchase?

Yes, within 14 days of the purchase, through support only. The full price goes back to your account in the Cabinet, and the bonus parts of that purchase are cancelled; you agree to this refund to your account. The Company makes the refund within 14 days of the withdrawal. A part joins the distribution of the profit only after those 14 days. (Buyer agreement, 5.4 and 8.1 to 8.3; Pre-purchase disclosure sheet, 8.2)

14. How do I get money back from my account?

Your account in the Cabinet is spendable only on parts of the Company's projects and is refunded only through support; it is never withdrawn at will. After a withdrawal from a purchase, on your request, support refunds the account by the means you paid, less the network fee for USDT. If your account is closed, money left in it is refunded through support. (Cabinet user agreement, 8.1 and 18.2; Buyer agreement, 8.2)

15. Can I sell or pass on my share?

A share passes only by inheritance, to the Company when no heir has claimed it within 10 years, or to the Company when it buys back every holder's share before a sale of a project's IP, at a price not below the share's value on the revaluation of the project made at the decision to sell; you are told in the Cabinet 30 days before the sale, and the price is paid to your account at the end of that notice. The Company promises no buy-back at your request. Transfer of a share between holders and a secondary market belong to version 2.0. If a holder dies, his heir may claim the share within 10 years of the death; until then the accruals are held on the deceased holder's account, and a share not claimed within those 10 years passes to the Company at the project's last valuation, the price being held for the heir as the law requires. The buyer agreement runs while the Company holds the project's right. (Buyer agreement, 1.5, 5.9 and 10.1 to 10.3)

16. How do I become an agent-integrator?

Pass identity verification (KYC) first: without it you cannot become an agent-integrator. Then press "Become an Integrator" in Settings; support staff review the application and give you the role; you then accept the agent-integrator agreement by a click. (Cabinet user agreement, 7.3, 10.1 and 10.2; Agent-integrator agreement, 2.1 and 2.2)
