Questions and Answers for a Project Account

1. What is a project account?

A project account is the account in the Cabinet through which a project of AIVIS.ONE LLC shows itself to buyers: its products, its news, its roadmap and its documents. Each project has one account. The Company's staff create the account and assign it.

2. What is on the Home tab?

Home shows the balance of the project account, with any amount on hold, the total of the project's sales in the Cabinet ("Total revenue" on the screen), the number of its products, and its recent activity in the Cabinet.

3. What does the Products tab show?

Products shows the project's products the way buyers see them in the store. A product is added or changed through support: write to support from Settings.

4. What does the Analytics tab show?

Analytics shows the project's sales in the Cabinet: the total of its sales ("Total revenue" on the screen), the sales of this month, sales by month as a chart, and sales by product, including products that have not sold yet.

5. How do I publish news?

In Settings, open Posts. A post has a title, a text, a cover image and tags. You keep it as a draft or publish it; a published post is shown to buyers on the project's page. You can edit or delete a post later.

6. How do I keep the roadmap?

In Settings, open Roadmap. An item is a milestone, an event or an announcement, with its title, description, dates, status and an optional link and cover image; buyers see the roadmap on the project's page. The type of an item cannot be changed after it is created, and a completed milestone cannot change its status.

7. How do I publish documents and images?

In Settings, open Attachments. You upload a file with its title, description, category and language, and choose whether buyers see it and whether it may be downloaded without signing in. A removed attachment is hidden everywhere.

8. Who approves what the project publishes?

Nobody: posts, the roadmap and attachments are published by the project itself, without approval. Each change is recorded in the project's activity history.

9. What is the activity history?

In Settings, open Activity. It lists the changes made to the project, newest first: what changed and whether it was made by you, by the Company's staff or by the system.

10. What is the balance of the project account?

Balance shows the amount available to withdraw and any amount on hold, the history of withdrawals and your payout details.

11. How do I request a withdrawal?

First set your payout details on the Balance tab. Then request a withdrawal of at least 10 USD and at most 100,000 USD, within the amount available. The Company's staff review every request; its status shows in the withdrawal history.

12. Can I change the price, the supply or the distribution?

Settings shows Pricing & supply and the distribution. Do not change the price or the supply from the project account: the price of a part changes only by a revaluation of the project, and no part is ever added; write to support. A change of the price also changes the price of every product of the project and removes their instalment plans. The distribution is managed by the Company's staff and cannot be changed from the project account.

13. How do I change the project's description and media?

In Settings, the Profile section holds the description and the links to the logo, the cover image, the promo video and the presentation. Edit them with the pencil next to the section title.

14. Can the project leave the store?

Yes. In Settings, Profile, "Withdraw from the store" hides the project from buyers at once. Only the Company's administrator can publish it again or archive it.

15. How do I protect the account?

In Settings, Account, you can turn on two-factor authentication, see the active sessions and sign out a device, and set which notifications you receive and quiet hours. "Deactivate account" signs you out on every device at once; to use the account again, write to support.

16. How do I reach support?

In Settings, open Support and write to the Company's staff there. The Company's terms for every user of the Cabinet are in the Cabinet user agreement.
