Questions and Answers for a Project Account

1. What is a project account?

A project account is the account in the Cabinet through which a project of AIVIS.ONE LLC shows itself to buyers: its products, its news, its roadmap and its documents. Each project has one account. The Company's staff create the account and assign it.

2. What is on the Home tab?

Home shows the balance of the project account, with any amount on hold, the project's pool (15 percent of the gross receipts from sales of its product, zero until the product sells), the money from the sales of its parts ("Sales of parts" on the screen), the number of parts sold and of products, and its recent activity with the way to all operations.

3. What does the Products tab show?

Products shows the project's products the way buyers see them in the store. A product is added or changed through support: write to support from Settings.

4. What does the Analytics tab show?

Analytics shows the sales of the project's parts in the Cabinet: their total ("Sales of parts" on the screen), the sales of this month, sales by month as a chart for the last 3, 6 or 12 months, and sales by product, including products that have not sold yet; "Export CSV" saves them as a file.

5. How do I publish news?

On the Publications tab, open Posts. A post has a title, a text, a cover image and tags. You keep it as a draft or publish it; a published post is shown to buyers on the project's page. You can edit or delete a post later. "Preview" shows the post as a buyer will see it before it is published.

6. How do I keep the roadmap?

On the Publications tab, open Roadmap. An item is a milestone, an event or an announcement, with its title, description, dates, status and an optional link and cover image; buyers see the roadmap on the project's page. The type of an item cannot be changed after it is created, and a completed milestone cannot change its status.

7. How do I publish documents and images?

On the Publications tab, open Documents. You upload a file with its title, description, category and language, and choose whether buyers see it and whether it may be downloaded without signing in. A removed attachment is hidden everywhere.

8. Who approves what the project publishes?

Nobody: posts, the roadmap and attachments are published by the project itself, without approval. Each change is recorded in the project's activity history.

9. What is the activity history?

In Settings, open Activity. It lists the changes made to the project, newest first: what changed and whether it was made by you, by the Company's staff or by the system.

10. What is the balance of the project account?

The balance stands in the top bar; tapping it opens the Balance screen: the amount available to withdraw and any amount on hold, a request in review, the history of withdrawals and the way to all operations. A withdrawal is used only inside AIVIS and changes no rights of the holders of parts.

11. How do I request a withdrawal?

On the Balance screen, tap "Request withdrawal" and give the network, the wallet address and the amount: at least 10 USD and at most 100,000 USD, within the amount available. The network and the wallet are kept for the next request. The Company's administrator reviews every request; a request in review is shown above the button and in the withdrawal history.

12. Can I change the price, the supply or the distribution?

No. Settings shows the price of a part and the parts for sale, read only: the price of a part is the fair value of the project divided by 1,000,000,000 and changes only by a revaluation of the project; 25 percent of the parts are for sale and no part is ever added. The distribution of the parts -- 25 percent for sale, 10 percent to the index, 14 percent bonus fund, 51 percent reserve -- is fixed and shown as a table.

13. How do I change the project's description and media?

In Settings, the Profile section holds the description and the links to the logo, the cover image, the promo video and the presentation. Edit them with the pencil next to the section title.

14. Can the project leave the store?

Yes. In Settings, Profile, "Withdraw from the store" hides the project from buyers at once. Only the Company's administrator can publish it again or archive it.

15. How do I protect the account?

In Settings, Account, you can turn on two-factor authentication, see the active sessions and sign out a device, and set which notifications you receive and quiet hours. "Deactivate account" signs you out on every device at once; to use the account again, write to support. A project cannot deactivate its account while one of its products is on sale.

16. How do I reach support?

In Settings, open Support and write to the Company's staff there. The Company's terms for every user of the Cabinet are in the Cabinet user agreement.
