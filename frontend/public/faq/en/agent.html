Questions and Answers for an Agent-Integrator

1. How do I become an agent-integrator?

Pass identity verification (KYC) first: without it you cannot become an agent-integrator. Then press "Become an Integrator" in Settings; support staff review the application and give you the role; you then accept the agent-integrator agreement by a click. An agent-integrator is a natural person. (Cabinet user agreement, 7.3, 10.1 and 10.2; Agent-integrator agreement, 2.1 and 2.2)

2. What does an agent-integrator do?

You sell parts of the projects of AIVIS.ONE LLC outside Georgia and support the three levels below you as a whole: you train their agent-integrators, tell them and their buyers about the projects and their products, answer their questions about the projects and the Cabinet, keep them informed in time, remind them of payments where needed and help them through verification. You tell buyers the plain state of the projects and promise them nothing that the Company has not published. The seller of the parts is the Company, and every purchase is made by the buyer himself in the Cabinet. (Agent-integrator agreement, 1.1 to 1.3 and 4.7)

3. Am I an employee of the Company?

No. You are an independent contractor: not an employee, partner or representative of the Company, with no authority to bind it and not on its payroll. You bear your own costs and risks, answer for any licence or registration that your country requires for your work, and pay your own taxes. (Agent-integrator agreement, 3.1, 3.2 and 11.1)

4. Which buyers are mine, and what are the three levels?

A buyer is yours by your own link, the one through which he registered in the Cabinet. The three levels below you are counted by the chain of registration links through agent-integrators, exactly three: your first level is the buyers who registered by your own link, your second level the buyers who registered by the link of an agent-integrator of your first level, and your third level the buyers who registered by the link of an agent-integrator of your second level. The chain ends at an agent-integrator who is blocked in the Cabinet: neither he nor anyone above him is paid on the purchases made below him. (Agent-integrator agreement, 4.1 to 4.3)

5. What commission am I paid?

A money commission on the purchases of parts made by the buyers of your three levels: 10 percent of the money paid for the parts at the first level, 3 percent at the second and 1 percent at the third. It accrues when the money for the purchase is paid; on an instalment purchase, on the money of each monthly payment that is paid. Where a buyer withdraws from a purchase within the 14 days or his payment is reversed, support cancels the commission from that purchase before it is paid. (Agent-integrator agreement, 5.1 to 5.4 and 8.1)

6. What are the volume pools?

Each month the 20 agent-integrators with the largest first-line volume of that month share 2 percent of all the month's purchases, and each quarter the 10 with the largest first-line volume of its three months share 1 percent of all the quarter's purchases, gifts excluded in both. Your first-line volume is the purchases of the buyers who registered by your own link. The two pools are counted separately, each is shared in proportion to each one's volume, and you may share in both. A purchase withdrawn within the 14 days, or whose payment is reversed, is left out. (Agent-integrator agreement, 6.1 to 6.4)

7. Do I receive bonus parts?

Yes, on a one-time purchase by a buyer of your first level, one who registered by your own link, by the amount of that purchase: 5 percent from 200 USD, 10 percent from 1,000 USD, 15 percent from 5,000 USD and 20 percent from 25,000 USD. Support credits them by hand from the project's bonus fund after the 14 days in which the buyer may withdraw; on an instalment purchase they are credited after its last payment, calculated on the amount paid. You hold them on the terms of the buyer agreement, entered in the project's register as their holder, and they carry the same share of the project's distributable profit as any other part. (Agent-integrator agreement, 7.1 to 7.3)

8. When and how am I paid?

Your commission is held in a separate withdrawal account, the Company's debt for the commission you have earned. You ask for a payout in the Cabinet, and support staff process it: a payout you ask for is paid 14 days after your request, the same 14 days in which a buyer may withdraw from a purchase. The Company approves every payout and does not pay a request that the balance no longer covers after a cancellation. A payout needs a passed verification. (Agent-integrator agreement, 8.1 to 8.5)

9. In what is the payout made, and to which wallet?

Your commission may be paid in USDT through the Cabinet or by bank in USD or EUR through support. For a payout in USDT, name the network and the wallet in your payout details. (Agent-integrator agreement, 8.3; Cabinet user agreement, 10.3)

10. What happens if my verification is withdrawn?

You are paid nothing until you pass verification again, and your accruals are kept. The Company may also end the agreement at once where your verification is withdrawn. A new verification is a new attempt with a new fee. (Agent-integrator agreement, 8.5 and 9.2; KYC notice, 5.3 and 6.3)

11. How does the agreement end?

It has no fixed term, and either party may end it by giving 30 days' written notice. When it ends, the balance of your withdrawal account that has not been cancelled is paid on your request through support, 14 days after the request; support pays an agent-integrator who is blocked in the Cabinet by hand. (Agent-integrator agreement, 9.1 and 9.3)

12. How do I reach support?

Write to support in the Cabinet: its staff process your payouts. You can also write to the Company at Georgia, Tbilisi, Mtatsminda district, village Okrokana, Akaki Tsereteli str., N14, or by e-mail to hello@aivis.one; legal and data questions go to legal@aivis.one, and Telegram is @aivisone. (Agent-integrator agreement, 8.3; Pre-purchase disclosure sheet, 8.4; Cabinet user agreement, 21.1)
