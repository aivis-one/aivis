K26 Action menu

The menu button pattern: a button that opens a list of actions (role=menu, role=menuitem), Down or Enter opens, the arrows, Home, End and a typed letter move, Escape closes and returns the focus to the button. Used for the share actions of a hub link (A-2) and the row actions of a staff list (S-2). Try it by keyboard below.

K32 Stepper and schedule list

Review and sign-up flows and the schedule list. An ordered list; the step in progress carries aria-current="step". A state is never colour alone: a check, a triangle or a number, and a word. The schedule list is a list of rows with a state each: a title and a Badge with the state in words; an item that needs attention is tinted.

Booking card, K44 Document row, K36 Document viewer

A position page: each purchase is one booking card with the date, the units, its basis in plain words, the agreement as HTML (opens the viewer) and the signed PDF as a document row, or a row that says plainly that the file is not uploaded yet. The document row is a real link with the type and size in its text. The viewer is a dialog with a labelled heading; the focus moves in, stays in, and returns to the button. The booking card writes the amount paid; nothing here writes the price of a part or a payment term.

K43 Invoice and QR block

A deposit page: the fields of the open invoice, the QR as an image with alt text, the same address as text, a Copy button with a polite confirmation. The QR and the address are left-to-right islands and the QR pad stays white in both themes (a scanner needs dark on white). The values are sample values; the QR picture is a sample pattern, not a real code.

K37 Bar chart with a text alternative

The analytics screen of a company (C-4): units by month, oldest first. The bars are an svg with role="img" and aria-labelledby; the same numbers are a table underneath, open by default, which is the text alternative. The highest bar has an outline as well as its fill, so the reading never rests on colour alone. The numbers are sample numbers.
