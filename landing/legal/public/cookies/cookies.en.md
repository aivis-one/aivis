# Cookie and Local Storage Notice

## 1. In short {#c-h-in-short}

1.1. The public pages of {site.domain} (the "Site") set no cookies of their own. They use your browser's local storage for one setting, the colour theme, and only after you press the theme button.

1.2. We run no analytics, no advertising and no third-party scripts on the public pages, so there is no consent banner. The only cookie that can reach your browser from them is a strictly necessary security cookie that {host.name} may set as our proxy (clause 4).

1.3. The cabinet at {site.domain}{site.path.app}/ (the "Cabinet") keeps in your browser only what it needs to work (clause 7).

## 2. What the Site stores {#c-h-what-the-site-stores}

2.1. The Site writes only one key to your device: aivis-theme, in the local storage of your browser. It holds one word, "light" or "dark", so that your next visit shows the theme you chose.

2.2. Our own page script writes the key when you press the theme button and reads it when a page loads. It stays until you delete it or clear the site's data, and it is never sent to a server.

## 3. What the public pages do not store {#c-h-what-the-public-pages-do-not-store}

3.1. The public pages:

- set no cookies of their own;
- use no session storage and no IndexedDB;
- use no analytics, advertising or tracking identifiers, and no fingerprinting;
- do not store your language: it is set by the page you open.

## 4. Server logs and the proxy {#c-h-server-logs-and-the-proxy}

4.1. The Site runs on the Company's own server, and traffic to it passes through {host.name} as a proxy for protection and speed. Our server and {host.name} receive each request and may keep standard operating logs: your IP address, the page requested, the time and your browser. These are not stored on your device. They are described in the [privacy policy]({site.path.privacy}).

4.2. Because the proxy stands between your browser and our server, {host.name} may set a cookie that is strictly necessary for security, for example to tell real visitors from automated attacks. Our pages do not set it and we do not control its content. It is not used for tracking or advertising, and it does not need your consent. You can delete it in your browser at any time.

## 5. Why no consent is asked {#c-h-why-no-consent-is-asked}

5.1. The theme key is stored only because you press the button, and only to give you what you asked for. We treat it as exempt from consent, as we treat the strictly necessary security cookie of the proxy.

5.2. A tool that needs consent, such as analytics or an embedded third-party widget, would need your consent first, and this notice would list it.

## 6. How to remove or block it {#c-h-how-to-remove-or-block-it}

6.1. Delete the key in your browser's site-data settings, or block local storage for this site. The public pages work fully without it. Without the key, the theme follows your device's light or dark setting on each visit.

## 7. The Cabinet {#c-h-the-cabinet}

7.1. The Cabinet sets no cookies of its own. It keeps in your browser only what it needs to work:

- the token that keeps you signed in: in the local storage of your browser when you use the Cabinet on the web, and in the session storage of the tab when you open it inside Telegram, where it is gone when you close the tab;
- the language and the colour theme you chose, in local storage;
- if you came through the link of an agent-integrator, the code of that link, in the session storage of the tab until you register or sign in;
- when a member of the Company's staff enters a user's account in a support session, the staff member's own sign-in, in the session storage of his tab until the support session ends.

7.2. The Cabinet's pages also load Telegram's Mini App script from telegram.org, so that the Cabinet can open inside Telegram. That script keeps a few technical keys (its start parameters and, inside Telegram, its display settings) in the session storage of your browser tab until you close the tab, and Telegram receives the request under its own policy.

7.3. All of this serves the service you asked for, and none of it is used for tracking or advertising. You can delete it in your browser's site-data settings; you will then have to sign in again.
