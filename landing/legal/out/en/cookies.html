Cookie and local storage notice

Updated 2 October 2026

In short

The public pages of aivis.one set no cookies. They use your browser’s local storage for one setting, the colour theme, and only after you press the theme button. We run no analytics, no advertising and no third-party scripts, so there is no consent banner.

What is stored

The site writes only this key to your device.

The key aivis-theme is kept in the local storage of your browser. It holds one word, “light” or “dark”, so that your next visit shows the theme you chose. Our own page script writes it when you press the theme button and reads it when a page loads. It stays until you delete it or clear the site’s data, and it is never sent to a server.

What is not stored

  • No cookies are set by our pages.
  • No session storage and no IndexedDB.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking identifiers, and no fingerprinting.
  • Your language is set by the page you open. It is not stored.

Server logs

The site runs on the company’s own server, and traffic to it passes through Cloudflare, Inc. as a proxy for protection and speed. Our server and Cloudflare, Inc. receive each request and may keep standard operating logs (your IP address, the page requested, the time, your browser). These are not stored on your device. They are described in the privacy policy.

Why no consent is asked

The theme key is stored only because you press the button, and only to give you what you asked for. We treat it as exempt from consent.

A tool that needs consent, such as analytics or an embedded third-party widget, would need your consent first, and this notice would list it.

How to remove or block it

Delete the key in your browser’s site-data settings, or block local storage for this site. The site works fully without it. Without the key, the theme follows your device’s light or dark setting on each visit.

The sign-in area

The sign-in area is a separate service and may store more, for example a session. It has its own notice.

Complaints

The supervisory authority for personal data is the State Audit Office of Georgia. See the privacy policy for your rights and how to use them.
