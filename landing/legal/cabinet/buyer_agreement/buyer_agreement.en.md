# Buyer Agreement

Made by acceptance in the Cabinet at {site.domain}{site.path.app}/; the Cabinet records the date and time of acceptance.

between

**{company.name}**, a limited liability company of Georgia, identification number {company.id}, legal address {company.address} (the "Company"),

and

the natural person whose verification (KYC) is passed and who accepts this agreement in the Cabinet from his own account (the "Buyer").

## 1. Subject {#ba-h1}

1.1. The Company will own the exclusive right to the computer program and the other works of each of its projects (the "IP") while the schedule for the project is unsigned, and owns it from the signing of the schedule. The Company sells parts of a project only after the schedule for the project is signed. Under this agreement the Company sells the Buyer, and the Buyer buys, parts of the exclusive right to a project (the "Parts"), in the quantity, for the price and of the project stated in the purchase.

1.2. What the Company sells is a share in an existing right that it owns at the moment of sale: a Part is one billionth (1/1,000,000,000) of the exclusive right to the project, and all the Parts of a project together equal 100 percent of the right. By buying Parts the Buyer becomes a co-owner of the exclusive right in the amount of his Parts and receives a share of the project's distributable profit, as clause 3 defines it. This agreement is a mixed contract: the sale of a share in the exclusive right to a project, together with the transfer to the Company of the management of that right (clause 9) and the distribution of the income from its use (clause 3).

1.3. A Part is not a share or equity in the Company, not a right to acquire a share of the Company, not an option and not a fund. The Holder's income is income from the use of the right that belongs to him, not income from an investment in the Company.

1.4. A purchase of units in the index project "AIVIS Projects Index" is a purchase under this agreement and is governed also by the Index Rules, which say what a unit is. In that project the Parts are called units. What the Index Rules state of the index -- what a unit is and the income it gives, the number of units, the price of a unit, the bonus fund, the use of the money paid for units, and the buy-out and closing of a project in the index -- applies to that project in place of clauses 1.2, 2.4, 3.1, 3.4, 3.6, 6.1 to 6.3 and 6.5, of the first sentence of clause 5.8 and of the second to fourth sentences of clause 5.9.

## 2. The register entry {#ba-h2}

2.1. The Company keeps in the Cabinet a register of the IP of each project: who holds which Parts of the exclusive right and how the project's distributable profit is distributed. The register is a digital system in which the exclusive right to the project is expressed in Parts.

2.2. The Buyer's right is his share in the exclusive right, expressed by the entry in this register. The Cabinet holds the proof of the entry.

2.3. After the purchase the Buyer is a holder of the Parts (the "Holder").

2.4. Each project has 1,000,000,000 Parts. The number is fixed and no Part is ever added.

## 3. The distributable profit {#ba-h3}

3.1. The co-owners of a project, the Holders and the Company, share the project's distributable profit in proportion to their Parts. The distributable profit is the project's gross receipts less the project's direct costs from the closed list of clause 3.3.

3.2. Gross receipts are all money received from customers for the project's product, before any deduction. The distributable profit is counted only on the sales of the project's product. The money from selling Parts is never gross receipts and does not enter the distributable profit.

3.3. The direct costs are these, and no others: payment-system and bank fees; hosting, servers and AI models that directly serve the project's product; third-party licences inside the product; taxes on the project's revenue, such as VAT; direct support of the product's customers. There are no other deductions. In particular, the development, salaries and marketing of the project are not deductions.

3.4. The Holder's income equals the project's distributable profit x his Parts / 1,000,000,000. The Company takes no commission: it earns from the project only as the holder of its own Parts.

3.5. Example, with figures chosen for illustration only. Gross receipts of 100,000 for a quarter and direct costs by the list of 20,000: the distributable profit is 80,000, and 1 percent of the Parts receives 800.

3.6. If the direct costs of a quarter exceed the gross receipts, the distributable profit of that quarter is zero; the Holder bears no loss, and the loss is not carried forward to a later quarter. Until the project's product sells, the project's gross receipts, and so its distributable profit, are zero.

## 4. Changes to the terms {#ba-h4}

4.1. Any change to what this agreement promises about the distributable profit, the closed list of direct costs and the payment calendar applies to Parts already sold only with the Holder's consent.

4.2. The distributable profit, the closed list of direct costs and the absence of a commission are shown to the Buyer before the purchase.

## 5. Who pays, and when {#ba-h5}

5.1. The Company pays the Holders their share of the distributable profit itself. It is the one debtor of the Holders, and the duty to pay them does not pass to anyone else.

5.2. The Company pays from a separate account of each project. Until a project's gross receipts reach 25,000 USD a month, the Company sells the project's product itself. At that cutoff the Company creates a subsidiary for the project, keeps the IP and licenses it to the subsidiary, which collects the gross receipts and sends the project's distributable profit to the Company. The fee under that licence is not a direct cost and does not reduce the distributable profit, which is counted for the project as a whole. The Company stays the one debtor, and nothing changes for the Holder.

5.3. Payments are quarterly, and only after the 14 days of withdrawal in clause 8. An amount under 10 USD is carried to the next quarter.

5.4. A Part joins the distribution of the profit only when the money paid for it can no longer be recalled, that is after those 14 days.

5.5. The Company pays the Holder to his bank account or card. A Holder who paid in USDT is paid in USDT to his confirmed address; for a payment in USDT, the Holder names the network and the wallet in his payout details. A payout needs a passed verification.

5.6. Each payment comes with a calculation of the Holder's share: the revenue, the direct costs by the list, the distributable profit and the Holder's share. Each payment also comes with a statement of the Holder's accruals and payments. The Cabinet shows accruals and payments only. It shows no balance that the Holder can spend or withdraw.

5.7. The Company holds every Part not yet sold or given, and the share of the distributable profit that falls to those Parts is directed to developing the projects.

5.8. The money from selling Parts goes to the budget of that project by its roadmap and business-financial model, less the selling cost. The Company has no sales department of its own; sales are made by agent-integrators, whose commissions are the selling cost, paid from the money for the Parts and disclosed to the Buyer before the purchase.

5.9. The Company stays the one debtor of the Buyer whatever happens to a project's IP. The Company may sell a project's IP only after it has bought back the share in the right of every Holder of that project, at a price not below the value of the share on the revaluation of the project made at the decision to sell. Every Holder is told in the Cabinet 30 days before the sale, and the price is paid to his account in the Cabinet at the end of that notice. The buy-out binds every Holder: on such a sale each Holder's share in the right passes to the Company at that price, and the Holder is paid for it.

## 6. The price and bonus Parts {#ba-h6}

6.1. The price of a Part is the project's fair valuation divided by 1,000,000,000. The same approach applies to every project.

6.2. Every project is revalued at least every six months until its product first sells, and at least every three months from then, justified by its formulas.

6.3. A project's value is never below 1,000,000 USD, which is its base until another valuation is made. News, work done or a deal move a price only through such a revaluation.

6.4. On a one-time purchase the Buyer receives bonus Parts by the amount of that purchase: 0 percent from 200 USD, 5 percent from 1,000 USD, 10 percent from 5,000 USD and 20 percent from 25,000 USD. No bonus Parts are given on an instalment purchase.

6.5. Bonus Parts come from the bonus fund of the project, 14 percent of its Parts. A Holder's bonus Parts carry the same share of the distributable profit as any other Part.

## 7. Purchase and acceptance {#ba-h7}

7.1. The Buyer accepts this agreement by a click in the Cabinet. The agreement and the Buyer's transactions are downloadable in the Cabinet.

7.2. Before the purchase the Company shows the Buyer the pre-purchase disclosure sheet.

7.3. A Buyer is a natural person after verification. Every Buyer is treated as a consumer and makes no declaration whether he is a consumer or a business.

7.4. The minimum purchase is 200 USD. An instalment purchase runs 6, 12 or 24 months on the fixed packages of 200, 1,000, 5,000 and 25,000 USD; any other amount or term only by agreement through support. No bonus Parts are given on an instalment purchase.

7.5. On an instalment purchase the price is paid in equal monthly payments; the last payment may differ by the remainder of rounding. Each paid month is a purchase agreement of its own and transfers that month's share of the Parts booked for the instalment purchase: on 6 months, 10 percent of the booked Parts for each of the first five months and 50 percent for the last; on 12 months, 5 percent for each of the first eleven months and 45 percent for the last; on 24 months, 3 percent for each of the first twenty-three months and 31 percent for the last.

7.6. Each monthly payment is taken from the Buyer's account in the Cabinet on its due date; a payment the account does not cover on that date is overdue. If the account covers it within 7 days of its due date, the instalment purchase continues. If it is not covered within those 7 days, the instalment purchase ends: the Parts of the months already paid stay the Buyer's, the booked Parts of the unpaid months go back to sale, and no penalty is charged.

7.7. Each document that the Cabinet generates for the Buyer -- the purchase agreement, the agreement of each paid instalment month, the ownership certificate and the gift certificate -- carries the facsimile signature of the Company's director and the Company's seal. The Buyer agrees that such documents are signed by the Company.

## 8. Withdrawal within 14 days {#ba-h8}

8.1. The Buyer may withdraw from the purchase within 14 days, by this agreement and through support only.

8.2. On withdrawal the full price goes back to the Buyer's single account in the Cabinet, and the bonus Parts of that purchase are cancelled. On the Buyer's request, support refunds the account out by the means by which he paid, less the network fee for USDT. The Buyer agrees to this refund to his account.

## 9. Management of the right, no partnership, no vote {#ba-h9}

9.1. The Holder is a co-owner of the exclusive right in the amount of his Parts. By this agreement the Holder passes to the Company the management of the right: licensing it, running the product, protecting the right and, after the buy-out of clause 5.9, selling the project's IP. The consent of each Holder is not required for these acts of the Company.

9.2. The Holder takes no part in managing the Company and no part in its obligations. The Holders are not partners of the Company.

9.3. The Holders have no vote, not even an advisory voice on the product.

## 10. The share {#ba-h10}

10.1. A share passes only by inheritance, or to the Company by the buy-out of clause 5.9. Nothing else can be done with the share. Transfer of a share between Holders and a secondary market belong to version 2.0.

10.2. The Company promises no buy-back at the Holder's request; the buy-out of clause 5.9 is made only before a sale of a project's IP.

## 11. Taxes {#ba-h11}

11.1. The Company settles its own taxes and the taxes of the project. The Holder settles his own taxes with his tax authority.

11.2. The Company withholds from a payment to the Holder only what the Tax Code makes compulsory.

## 12. Forum {#ba-h12}

12.1. A dispute under this agreement goes to the state courts of Georgia in Tbilisi, unless mandatory law where he lives gives him the right to go to another forum. The Buyer's protection as a consumer at his home is kept.

12.2. The Buyer may sign a separate agreement for arbitration at GIAC after a dispute arises.

12.3. This agreement is governed by the law of Georgia.

## 13. Language and general {#ba-h13}

13.1. The English text of this agreement prevails over its text in any other language.

13.2. A change to this agreement applies to Parts already sold only as clause 4 allows, that is with the Holder's consent.
