# Licence purchase agreement

## 1. Parties and subject {#la-h-parties}

This agreement is made between {company.name}, {company.address} (identification number {company.id}) (the Company), and the buyer who confirms a purchase of a licence in the Cabinet (the Buyer). The Buyer may be a business or a private person. The Company grants the Buyer a licence to use {sankord.name} (the Licence) on the terms below, and the Buyer pays for it. The Cabinet terms of use and the [privacy policy]({site.path.privacy}) apply to the purchase as well.

## 2. When this agreement applies {#la-h-when}

{sankord.plan} This agreement applies to a purchase made once licences are on sale. Before then no licence is sold and no payment is taken in advance.

## 3. The Licence {#la-h-grant}

The Licence follows the size that the Buyer declares at the purchase, which is declared once. The Company never counts the Buyer’s agents, runs or processes, and never limits them.

{sankord.name} runs inside the Buyer’s walls, on the Buyer’s own servers or in the Buyer’s own cloud account. Connections to services that have an API come with the Licence; there is no paid integration. Inside a company that has a Licence, another workplace may be added on the terms shown in the Cabinet.

When the Licence has been paid for in full, it is perpetual: it is the Buyer’s for good. A missed payment switches nothing off: the path to the perpetual Licence simply waits for the next payment. The Company keeps the ownership of the software and of the names {sankord.name} and {company.brand}; the Licence gives the right to use and nothing more.

A Buyer who is paying receives the major versions and the integrator’s support. A Buyer who is not paying receives security fixes only. Security fixes are free for every version. New versions are the Buyer’s choice.

## 4. Price and payment {#la-h-price}

The price of the Licence and the terms of payment are those shown in the Cabinet at the purchase and agreed in the order. This agreement sets neither. Payments are made by the means the Cabinet offers and are recorded in the Buyer’s account. The Licence is not a subscription.

## 5. Verification and AML {#la-h-kyc}

A purchase can be made only after the Buyer has passed identity verification, as the KYC notice describes. The Company may refuse a sale, or hold or reverse a payment, where its AML policy requires it.

## 6. Delivery and start {#la-h-start}

The Buyer starts on his own, with a guide agent that leads him step by step, or with an integrator. An integrator who sets up or supports {sankord.name} for the Buyer is an independent contractor, not a representative of the Company. What the integrator bills for his own work is a matter between him and the Buyer.

## 7. Support {#la-h-support}

While the Buyer is paying, his integrator answers him within two working days. Support works from an error file that the Buyer sends. Security fixes are free for every version. A Buyer who bought directly from the Company is supported by the Company’s own support, which answers within two working days.

## 8. Refund and mandatory rights {#la-h-rights}

The demo version is the trial: the Buyer may try it before buying. Once {sankord.name} has been installed for the Buyer, the price is not refunded. A consumer keeps the right to withdraw from the purchase that the law gives him, and nothing in this agreement limits another right that the Buyer has by law and that cannot be waived.

## 9. Liability {#la-h-liab}

To the extent Georgian law allows, and except in cases of intent or gross negligence, the Company is not liable for indirect loss arising from the Buyer’s use of {sankord.name}. Nothing in this agreement limits a liability that the law does not allow to be limited.

## 10. Governing law and disputes {#la-h-law}

This agreement is governed by the law of Georgia. A dispute with a consumer goes to the state courts of Tbilisi, Georgia, unless mandatory law where the consumer lives gives him the right to go to another forum. Any other dispute arising out of or in connection with this agreement is finally resolved by arbitration at the Georgian International Arbitration Centre in Tbilisi, in accordance with its arbitration rules.

## 11. Changes, language and contact {#la-h-change}

The Company may change this agreement for purchases made after the change; the text valid at the purchase is the one recorded in the Buyer’s purchase documents. The English text of this agreement prevails over its text in any other language. {company.name}, {company.address}. E-mail {contact.general}; legal questions {contact.legal}.
