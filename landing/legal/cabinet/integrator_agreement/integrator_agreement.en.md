# Integrator agreement

## 1. Parties and subject {#ia-h-parties}

This agreement is made between {company.name}, {company.address} (identification number {company.id}) (the Company), and the buyer whom the Company has approved as an integrator in the Cabinet (the Integrator). The Integrator sets up and supports {sankord.name} for other buyers, and the Company pays him a share of the payments of the buyers he brings, on the terms below.

## 2. Independent contractor {#ia-h-status}

The Integrator is an independent contractor. He is not an employee, agent, partner or representative of the Company, he has no authority to bind it, and he is not on its payroll. He bears his own taxes, costs and risks. He bills the buyers himself for his own work, and what he bills is a matter between him and the buyer. This agreement gives him no territory.

## 3. Becoming and remaining an integrator {#ia-h-become}

A buyer may apply to become an integrator once he has passed identity verification. The Company decides on the application. To become and to remain an integrator, the Integrator:

- owns a licence that he has bought from the Company;
- completes the training and passes the examinations that the Cabinet sets, and is certified before his first installation for a buyer;
- keeps his qualification, which the Company checks.

## 4. What the Integrator does {#ia-h-duties}

The Integrator sets up and supports {sankord.name} for the buyers he has brought. While a buyer is paying, the Integrator answers him within two working days; support works from the error file that the buyer sends, and security fixes are free for every version. The Integrator answers to the Company for this promise, which the Company has made to the buyer. The Company checks the quality of his work. He tells buyers the plain state of the product and promises them nothing that the Company has not published. Every decision in a buyer’s company stays with the buyer.

## 5. Share {#ia-h-share}

The Company pays the Integrator 10 percent of the licence payments made by the buyers he has brought and of the cash part of the extra workplaces those buyers pay for. A buyer is counted as brought by the Integrator when he registers through the Integrator’s link. The share is paid on the first line only: on the payments of the buyers the Integrator himself brought, and on no payment of a buyer brought by anyone else, including a buyer brought by one of the Integrator’s buyers.

The Cabinet records the share, and the Company pays it after the buyer’s payment has cleared. The Integrator may ask for it to be paid out, and the Company’s staff approve the payout. The Integrator who stops supporting a buyer stops receiving his share from that buyer’s payments, and the buyer moves to another integrator.

## 6. The Integrator’s own licence {#ia-h-licence}

The Integrator buys his own licence under the licence purchase agreement, on the same terms as any buyer.

## 7. Term and ending {#ia-h-end}

This agreement has no fixed term. Either party may end it by giving 30 days’ written notice. The Company may also end it, and the role, at once where the Integrator breaks it, loses his qualification, or where the AML policy or a sanctions rule requires it. When the agreement ends, the Integrator’s buyers move to another integrator.

## 8. Liability {#ia-h-liab}

To the extent Georgian law allows, and except in cases of intent or gross negligence, each party is liable to the other only for direct loss. The Company does not promise the Integrator any income.

## 9. Governing law and disputes {#ia-h-law}

This agreement is governed by the law of Georgia. Any dispute arising out of or in connection with it is finally resolved by arbitration at the Georgian International Arbitration Centre in Tbilisi, in accordance with its arbitration rules.

The English text of this agreement prevails over its text in any other language. {company.name}, {company.address}. E-mail {contact.general}; legal questions {contact.legal}.
