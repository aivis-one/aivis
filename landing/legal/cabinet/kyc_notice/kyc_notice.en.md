# Identity verification (KYC) notice

## 1. What this notice is {#kn-h-what}

Nothing can be bought in the Cabinet before you have passed identity verification, called KYC (“know your customer”). This notice tells you what you send, what it costs, who looks at it, what happens to it and what follows. It is read together with the [privacy policy]({site.path.privacy}).

## 2. Who is responsible {#kn-h-who}

The controller of the data you send is {company.name}, {company.address} (identification number {company.id}). You can write to the director, {company.director}, at {contact.legal}.

## 3. When it is needed {#kn-h-when}

Verification is mandatory before any purchase in the Cabinet. It is also a condition of applying to become an integrator. You may register, enter and look inside the Cabinet without it.

## 4. What you send {#kn-h-data}

- The type of your identity document: a passport, an ID card or a driving licence.
- A photograph of the front of the document and, except for a passport, of its back.
- A photograph of yourself.

Each file must be a JPEG or PNG image of up to 10 MB. Send only the document you are verified by, and only your own.

## 5. Why, and who decides {#kn-h-why}

We use these files to confirm who you are and that we may deal with you under the Company’s AML policy and the rules on sanctions. The basis is our legitimate interest in knowing who we sell to, our duty of care in payments, and your own request to buy.

Our staff review every submission by hand and decide. No automated decision is taken. The result is that you are verified (approved) or not (rejected), and a verification may later be withdrawn (revoked), for example when the documents turn out to be false or when the AML policy requires it.

## 6. The fee {#kn-h-fee}

Verification is paid and costs {kyc.fee}. The fee is paid from your balance in the Cabinet when you submit your documents, before staff look at them. It buys one verification session. The fee is never returned, whatever the result and whether or not you go on to buy anything; we say so openly here, before you pay.

While your submission is waiting for a decision you may come and go; you are not charged twice. If a verification is rejected or revoked, a new verification needs a new fee. Once you are approved, you pay nothing more for it.

## 7. Who sees your documents {#kn-h-staff}

Only the Company’s staff can open your documents, and the links by which they open them stop working after a few minutes. The files are kept on the Company’s own server. {host.name} carries them as a proxy while they travel to the server. The [privacy policy]({site.path.privacy}) names the others who handle data for the Cabinet.

## 8. How long we keep them {#kn-h-ret}

We keep your documents and photographs while your account exists. When your account is closed, we delete them, except what the law requires us to keep. The [privacy policy]({site.path.privacy}) states the rule for all data.

## 9. Your rights {#kn-h-rights}

You have the rights set out in the [privacy policy]({site.path.privacy}), including to see what we hold about you and to ask us to correct, delete or block it. Where the law requires us to keep a record, we keep it and tell you so.

## 10. Changes and language {#kn-h-change}

When we change this notice, we replace the text and show the line “Updated” with the date at the top. The English text of this notice prevails over its text in any other language.
