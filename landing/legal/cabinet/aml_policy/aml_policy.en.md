# Anti-money-laundering policy

## 1. The Company and this policy {#am-h-scope}

{company.name} (the Company) adopts this short policy voluntarily, as a matter of good practice, and applies it to the payments made in the Cabinet. The Company reviews it again before sales begin.

Details of the Company: {company.name}, identification number {company.id}, legal address: {company.address}; Director {company.director}; e-mail {contact.legal}; website {site.domain}.

The Company does not wish to be involved in unlawful acts connected with the laundering of criminal proceeds or the financing of terrorism, and deals with the payer openly and transparently.

## 2. Who is a payer {#am-h-payer}

In this policy a payer is a person who makes a payment to the Company in the Cabinet: a buyer paying for a licence, a person paying the fee for identity verification, or an integrator buying his licence.

## 3. How payments are made {#am-h-pay}

A payment is made by the means the Cabinet offers, such as a bank transfer or a payment in digital assets through the Company’s payment service. The Company records every payment in the Cabinet with its amount, currency, time and means. The details to pay to are given through the Cabinet.

## 4. Identification {#am-h-id}

Nothing is sold in the Cabinet before the payer has passed identity verification, described in the KYC notice. The Company may ask for further information about a payer or a payment.

## 5. When the Company does not accept a payment {#am-h-refuse}

The Company does not accept a payment and does not continue dealing with a payer if the payer cannot be identified, the information provided is untrue, or the operation involves a person included in a restrictive list. In such a case the Company may hold or reverse a payment, refuse a sale and close the account.

## 6. Countries {#am-h-countries}

The Cabinet is not offered in the countries named in its terms of use, and the Company does not deal with payers there.

## 7. Updates {#am-h-change}

The Company updates this policy when that is needed. The English text of this policy prevails over its text in any other language.
