# AML and Screening Policy

## 1. The Company and this policy {#aml-h1}

1.1. **{company.name}**, a limited liability company of Georgia, identification number {company.id}, legal address {company.address} (the "Company"), adopts this policy of its own accord as its own screening policy. It applies to every payment made to the Company or by it through the Cabinet at {site.domain}{site.path.app}/ or through support. It is the screening policy that the Cabinet user agreement refers to.

1.2. The Company does not wish to be involved in the laundering of criminal proceeds or the financing of terrorism, and deals with every payer openly and transparently.

1.3. The Company's support staff carry out this policy by hand.

## 2. Payers and payees {#aml-h2}

2.1. A payer is a person who pays the Company in the Cabinet: a buyer paying for parts of the projects of {company.name}, a monthly payment of an instalment purchase included, or a person paying the verification fee.

2.2. A payee is a person whom the Company pays: a holder paid his accruals, or an agent-integrator paid his commission.

## 3. Identification and screening {#aml-h3}

3.1. Nothing is bought before the buyer has passed the paid identity verification (KYC) with documents. A purchase, an instalment, a payout of money and an application to become an agent-integrator need a passed verification. The KYC notice describes the verification.

3.2. Staff screen every person at verification against the sanctions lists of the United States, the European Union, the United Kingdom and the United Nations.

3.3. The Company refuses persons on these lists and residents of Cuba, Iran, North Korea, Syria, Crimea, DNR and LNR. Russian and Belarusian citizens are accepted after screening against the lists.

3.4. The screening applies to every buyer, whoever brought him, an agent-integrator included.

3.5. The Company may ask for further information about a payer or a payment.

## 4. How payments are made to the Company {#aml-h4}

4.1. A payer can pay in USDT in the Cabinet, on the networks TRC20, ERC20 and BSC20, or by bank transfer in USD or EUR through support.

4.2. USDT is taken only as payment for the Company's own goods and services and is credited as internal settlement units. The Company holds no crypto for others and shows no crypto account in the Cabinet, and nothing is given back as crypto at will.

4.3. A USDT deposit is accepted before verification, and the verification fee may itself be paid in USDT.

4.4. Bank details of the Company are given by support only to a client whose verification is passed, at support's decision.

4.5. No bank transfer is accepted from Russian buyers, a Russian resident in the European Union included. They pay in USDT and are not given bank details.

4.6. The Company records every payment in the Cabinet with its amount, currency, time and means, the references the payment service gives, and, for USDT, the address from which it was sent.

## 5. Payments by the Company {#aml-h5}

5.1. A payout of money needs a passed verification. An agent-integrator whose verification is withdrawn is paid nothing until he passes it again, and his accruals are kept.

5.2. The Company pays a holder his accruals to his bank account or card, or, where the holder paid in USDT, in USDT to his confirmed address.

5.3. An agent-integrator's commission is paid in USDT through the Cabinet or by bank in USD or EUR through support, 14 days after his request; the Company's support holds the payout for these 14 days. Where a buyer withdraws from a purchase within the 14 days or his payment is reversed, support cancels the agent-integrators' commission from that purchase before it is paid.

5.4. A buyer's account in the Cabinet is spendable only on parts of the Company's projects and is refunded only through support; it is never withdrawn at will. On a withdrawal within 14 days the full price goes back to the buyer's account; on his request support refunds the account out by the means by which he paid, less the network fee for USDT.

## 6. When the Company does not accept a payment {#aml-h6}

6.1. The Company does not accept a payment and does not go on dealing with a payer where the payer cannot be identified, where the information given is untrue, or where the payer is a person the Company refuses under clause 3.3. In such a case the Company may refuse a sale, refuse or withdraw a verification and close the account.

6.2. If a payer makes a USDT deposit but does not pass verification, the Company, except in the case of clause 6.4, refunds the deposit within 90 days, less the verification fee where that fee was taken from this deposit. The refund goes to the sending address only and is made less the network fee.

6.3. The Company may reverse a payment that was recorded in error or was not completed by the payment service; a mistake is corrected by a reversing entry.

6.4. Money received from a person found on a sanctions list is held as the law requires and is not refunded.

## 7. Records {#aml-h7}

7.1. The Company keeps the records of payments, payouts and verification for the periods set out in the privacy policy.

## 8. Changes and language {#aml-h8}

8.1. When the Company changes this policy, it replaces the text and shows the line "Updated" with the date at the top.

8.2. The English text of this policy prevails over its text in any other language.
