# Privacy Policy

## 1. In short {#p-h-in-short}

1.1. This policy covers the public pages of aivis.one (the home page, About, Courses and Questions) and the Cabinet at {site.domain}{site.path.app}/, where registered users buy parts of the projects of {company.name} and see their register entry, accruals, payments and documents. It is the one privacy policy for the site and the Cabinet, and the privacy page of the site shows this text.

1.2. The public pages run no analytics, load no third-party scripts, have no sign-up or contact forms, and our own pages set no cookies on them. You reach us by e-mail or Telegram, and then we hold what you send us.

1.3. The Cabinet is different. To register, verify your identity, buy and be paid, we hold your account data, the documents and photographs you send for identity verification, the records of your payments and your entry in the register of the project. Our staff can see them in the way described below.

## 4. What data we handle and why {#p-h-what-data-we-handle-and-why}

We handle the following data, grouped by where you meet us. Each group names the reason and the basis.

### 4.1. The public pages {#p-h-the-public-pages}

4.1.1. When you open a page, the technical data that any web server receives reach the Company's server through Cloudflare: your IP address, the page requested, the date and time, browser and device details, and the page you came from. We use them to deliver the site and keep it secure and running. The basis is our legitimate interest in running a secure site. They stay in the operating logs of Cloudflare and of the server. We do not export or analyse these logs.

4.1.2. When you write to us by e-mail, we receive your e-mail address, your name if you give it, and whatever you write or attach. We use them to read and answer your message. The basis is your request and our legitimate interest in answering.

4.1.3. When you write to us on Telegram, we receive your Telegram name, username and ID, and whatever you write or send. We use them to read and answer your message. The basis is your request and our legitimate interest in answering. We keep them as we keep e-mail.

4.1.4. When you press the light/dark theme button, one word, "light" or "dark", is saved in your own browser. It is never sent to us. It is used to show the theme you chose the next time you visit. The basis is your own action. It stays in your browser until you delete it. See the [cookie and local storage notice]({site.path.cookies}).

4.1.5. You do not have to give us any data through the public pages. If you do not write to us, we receive nothing from you except the technical data a web server needs to answer your request.

### 4.2. The Cabinet: your account {#p-h-the-cabinet-your-account}

4.2.1. When you register, we receive the e-mail address you register with, or your Telegram name, username and ID if you sign in through Telegram, and the language you choose. In your profile you may give your first and last name, your country and your telephone number. We use them to create and run your account, to sign you in, to send you messages about it, and to show you the documents you have accepted. The basis is the user agreement between you and the Company and our legitimate interest in running the Cabinet securely.

4.2.2. If you come through the link of an agent-integrator, we record which link you used. We use it to work out the three levels and the commissions of the agent-integrators. The basis is our agreement with the agent-integrator and our legitimate interest in running that scheme correctly.

4.2.3. We record the documents you accept, with the version and the time. The basis is our legal interest in proving what was agreed.

### 4.3. The Cabinet: identity verification {#p-h-the-cabinet-identity-verification}

4.3.1. Nothing can be bought in the Cabinet before you pass identity verification. For verification we receive the type of your identity document (passport, ID card or driving licence), photographs of its front and, except for a passport, its back, and a photograph of yourself. Our staff look at the files and decide by hand; no automated decision is taken.

4.3.2. We use them to confirm who you are and that we may deal with you under our screening policy, which includes checking you against the sanctions lists. The basis is our legitimate interest in knowing who we sell to, our duty of care in payments, and your request to buy. If you hold a share, we keep the documents and photographs as long as your register entry; the basis for that is the buyer agreement and then our legitimate interest in proving the right and every payment. The KYC notice says what the check costs and what happens to the result.

### 4.4. The Cabinet: payments in {#p-h-the-cabinet-payments-in}

4.4.1. When you pay, we receive and keep the amount, the currency, the time, the payment method, the references the payment service gives us, the address from which a USDT payment was sent, and the state of the payment. We use them to credit your payments, to show them to you, to refund a payment to the sending address where the user agreement provides for it, to keep the Company's books and to meet our legal duties. The basis is the user agreement, the agreement for your purchase and our legal duties of accounting.

### 4.5. The Cabinet: your purchases and your register entry {#p-h-the-cabinet-your-purchases-and-your-register-entry}

4.5.1. When you buy, we record what you bought, in what quantity, for what price, with the buyer agreement you accepted, and we enter you in the Company's register of the project's IP as a holder. We use the entry to prove your right, to count your share of the pool and to show you your accruals and payments. The basis is the buyer agreement. We keep the entry and this record as section 8 says; the basis for keeping them after the share ends is our legitimate interest in proving the right and every payment.

### 4.6. The Cabinet: payments out {#p-h-the-cabinet-payments-out}

4.6.1. When the Company pays you your accruals, or an agent-integrator his commission, we hold the details needed to pay: your bank account or card, or for a holder who paid in USDT the confirmed USDT address, together with the amounts, the dates and the statement of each payment. The basis is the buyer agreement or the agent-integrator agreement and our legal duties of accounting.

### 4.7. The Cabinet: agent-integrators {#p-h-the-cabinet-agent-integrators}

4.7.1. If you apply to become an agent-integrator, we hold your application, the buyers attributed to you, the commissions due to you, your withdrawal account and the details needed to pay you. The basis is the agent-integrator agreement. Staff approve the application, and only after your identity verification is passed. If you receive bonus parts, we enter you in the register as their holder, as clause 4.5.1 says.

### 4.8. The Cabinet: messages and records of use {#p-h-the-cabinet-messages-and-records-of-use}

4.8.1. We send you messages about your account and purchases: confirmation of your address, the result of verification, payments, changes to the documents. We send no marketing messages. If you ask for help through the Cabinet, we keep your request and our answer.

4.8.2. We keep a log of important actions in the Cabinet, such as signing in, changes to your account, payments and every action of our staff in your account, with the time, your IP address and your browser details. We use it to keep the Cabinet secure, to solve problems and to show what happened. The basis is our legitimate interest in security and in being able to prove what was done.

### 4.9. What we do not do {#p-h-what-we-do-not-do}

4.9.1. We do not sell data, build profiles of visitors or users, or take any decision about you by automated means alone. We run no advertising and no analytics on the public pages.

## 5. Who can see your data in the Cabinet {#p-h-who-can-see-your-data-in-the-cabinet}

5.1. Only the Company's staff can see what is in the Cabinet, and each person only what the work needs. Staff can see your account data, your payments and your register entry, review the documents and photographs you sent for verification, reverse a payment, approve or refuse an agent-integrator's application and process a payout. The links through which staff open verification documents stop working after a few minutes.

5.2. To help you, staff may also enter your account in a support session, as if they were you. Every such session and every action in it is recorded in the log with the member of staff, the time and the account. Staff use it only to solve a problem you have raised or to protect the Cabinet.

## 6. Who receives data {#p-h-who-receives-data}

6.1. The site and the Cabinet run on the Company's own server. Traffic to them passes through Cloudflare as a proxy for protection and speed, so Cloudflare sees technical data such as your IP address, the page requested, the time and your browser, and also the traffic of the Cabinet while it passes. For us it acts as a processor of this data, under its published [data processing addendum](https://www.cloudflare.com/cloudflare-customer-dpa/).

6.2. Other services handle data for the Cabinet on our instruction. An e-mail delivery service sends the messages of the Cabinet. Telegram carries your sign-in if you use it and the notices you choose. The Cabinet's pages also load Telegram's Mini App script from telegram.org, so that the Cabinet can open inside Telegram; Telegram receives that request under its own policy. The payment service we use receives the details of a payment to process it. Our banks receive the details of a bank payment to or from you. Our legal advisers see data when we need advice. Our staff are described above.

6.3. If you write by e-mail or Telegram, your message passes through your provider, through our mailbox provider and through Telegram, which handle it under their own terms.

6.4. We pass your data to no one else, unless the law requires it or a competent authority lawfully asks for it.

6.5. The pages link to other services, such as Telegram, LinkedIn, X, Medium and our product site sankord.com. Once you follow a link you are on a service we do not run, and its own policy applies.

## 7. Transfers abroad {#p-h-transfers-abroad}

7.1. We work from Georgia. Cloudflare handles traffic through servers in many countries, and our e-mail, Telegram, payment and banking providers may store data outside Georgia. The countries where your data is stored are not fixed.

## 8. How long we keep it {#p-h-how-long-we-keep-it}

8.1. We keep data only as long as the purpose needs it, then delete or anonymise it, unless a law requires us to keep it.

- Ordinary correspondence: as long as it is needed for our talks with you and to protect our rights, and then we delete it.
- Your account and its documents: while the account exists, and afterwards only what the law requires us to keep.
- Identity documents and photographs: if you hold a share, as long as your register entry; if you never held a share, while your account exists, and when it is closed we delete them, except what the law requires us to keep.
- Payment records, payout statements and the agreements you accepted: as long as the share they concern exists, and for 10 years after it ends; those that concern no share, for the accounting period the law sets.
- Your register entry: as long as the right it records exists, and for 10 years after it ends, so that you or your heirs can always prove the right and every payment.
- Logs of the Cabinet: as long as they are needed for security and to show what was done.
- Backups of the Cabinet are kept for seven days and then overwritten.
- Server logs of the public pages follow the schedule of Cloudflare and of our server.
- The theme setting stays in your browser until you delete it.

## 9. Your rights {#p-h-your-rights}

9.1. Under the Law you have these rights:

- to ask whether we handle data about you, and to see it and receive a copy;
- to ask us to correct, update or complete it;
- to ask us to stop handling it;
- to ask us to delete or block it;
- to withdraw any consent you gave;
- not to be made subject to a decision taken only by automated means that has legal or similarly significant effect on you. We take no such decision.

9.2. We answer your request within 10 working days of receiving it. For information and copies the Law allows a further 10 working days in special cases.

9.3. To use a right, write to us at {contact.legal}, or through the Cabinet. To protect you, we may ask you to confirm who you are before we act. Where the law requires us to keep a record, such as an accounting record or your register entry, we keep it and tell you so.

9.4. You may complain to the supervisory authority, the [{authority.name}]({authority.url}). Its decisions can be appealed to a court.

## 10. Protection of data {#p-h-protection-of-data}

10.1. The public pages are static and we keep no database of visitors. The Cabinet does keep a database of its users, with the data described above. We protect it by limiting access to staff by their role, by signed-in sessions, by logging staff actions and by the short life of the links to verification documents.

10.2. As the Law requires, we record a data incident and notify the State Audit Office not later than 72 hours after identifying it, except where the incident poses a minimal threat to fundamental rights and freedoms.

## 11. Changes {#p-h-changes}

11.1. When we change this policy, we replace the text here and show the line "Updated" with the date at the top of this page.

## 12. Visitors and users in the EU and the UK {#p-h-visitors-and-users-in-the-eu-and-the-uk}

12.1. This site and the Cabinet are written in English and in Russian for readers worldwide and for Russian speakers outside Russia. Whether they are aimed at the EU or the UK, and so whether the GDPR or the UK GDPR applies to them, is not settled. We have not appointed a representative in the EU or the UK.

12.2. If the GDPR or the UK GDPR applies to you anyway, the same practice holds. Our grounds are the performance of our agreement with you, our legal obligations, and our legitimate interest in running a secure site and Cabinet, in knowing who we sell to and in answering messages. You have the rights of access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, and you may complain to your national data protection authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office).
